Lesson 0023

Choose the right visual (and when to go paginated)

VisualizeAbout 18 minAfter this lesson you can match an analysis need to a visual type, configure KPI and gauge wells, and spot when a paginated report is required.

Why this matters for the exam

Visual selection is a large share of the Visualize and analyze the data domain, and it appears as short scenario questions: "which visual shows...". The answers follow a handful of rules, plus two favourite traps: the KPI trend axis and printable reports.

Recall first

Name two ways to shrink an Import model.

Remove unused columns and unneeded rows in Power Query; split or reduce high-cardinality columns such as DateTime. Hiding does not shrink it.

Visualization types; waterfall; scatter; small multiples.Prefer bar charts over pie charts when comparing many categories accurately.

KPI visual: Value is the actual, Trend axis is time, Target is the goal.KPI. The trend axis needs a unique period such as Year-Quarter. Set direction (High is good or Low is good). The goal shows blank when its grain does not match the trend axis. A gauge shows one value against min, max and target, with no trend.Radial gauge. Forecast and anomaly detection on a line chart need a continuous date axis (lesson 0030).

Paginated reports (Power BI Report Builder, .rdl) are pixel-perfect and printable, print all rows across pages, export to PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint, and contain a tablix. Interactive Power BI reports suit exploration.Paginated or Power BI report?.

Practical example

Business context: the board pack needs a monthly invoice PDF for every customer with all line items, and executives also want to explore sales by region.

  • Invoices, all rows, print and PDF: a paginated report with a tablix, built in Report Builder.
  • Executive exploration: an interactive Power BI report with a bar chart by region and a line chart by month.
  • Quarterly sales against target: a KPI with Value = Sales, Trend axis = Year-Quarter, Target = Sales Target.

Why it works: each choice follows the requirement's verb (print every row, explore, track against a goal) instead of the visual that looks nicest.

Traps

Wrong belief: A pie chart suits ten categories or comparing margins across regions.
Correct rule: Pies work for a few slices. Bars compare many categories accurately.

Wrong belief: Quarter alone is a fine KPI trend axis.
Correct rule: It groups every year's Q1 together and sums across years. Use a unique Year-Quarter.

Wrong belief: An interactive table can serve a printable 40-page list.
Correct rule: It truncates. Go paginated.
