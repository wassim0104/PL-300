Six-week study plan

6 weeks6–8 h a weekPrintable checklist

Six weeks, 6–8 hours a week. About 60–70 minutes a day, six days a week; day 7 is the checkpoint. Ticks are saved in this browser.

Compress or extend

Week 1 = 0001–0011, week 2 = 0012–0022, week 3 = 0023–0036, week 4 = mocks 1 and 2 plus remediation. Keep the 1/3/7-day reviews; drop the 14-day ones.

Use the 6-week lesson split over weeks 1–6 at 5–6 lessons a week, week 7 = mock 1 + remediation, week 8 = mock 2 + final review. Add a DAX-only practice block in week 4.

Readiness rule

You are ready when two consecutive mock exams score 80% or more. Mocks draw 14 PREP, 14 MODEL, 14 VIZ and 8 MANAGE questions out of 50. Weighted by the official outline, see exam overview.
