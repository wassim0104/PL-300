Lesson 0025

Filters, slicers and sync slicers

Visualize and analyzeAbout 18 minSkill: choose the filter scope

After this lesson you can pick the filter scope (visual, page, report, slicer or synced slicer) that applies a selection to exactly the visuals and pages required.

Why this matters for the exam

The exam loves scope puzzles: “filter pages 2 and 3 but not page 4”. They are quick marks once you know which tool has which reach. Slicer and filter questions appeared repeatedly across the practice tests, and they feed the Visualize domain, which is about 25–30% of PL-300.

Core idea

Power BI offers five scopes. Pick by reach.Source: Add filters to a report.

ScopeReachesTypical use
Visual-level filterOne visualTop N, for example top 20 customers
Page-level filterAll visuals on one pageFixed page scope
Report-level filterAll pagesAlways exclude a category
SlicerIts page, by default; consumers change itInteractive choice on the canvas
Synced slicerThe pages you tickOne choice carried across chosen pages

Drillthrough filters are set on the target page (Lesson 0026).

Sync slicers

Open View › Sync slicers. Two checkboxes per page do different jobs: Sync shares the selection with that page, Visible shows or hides the slicer there.Source: Slicers in Power BI. A slicer can filter a page without being visible on it.

Sync groups (Advanced options) connect slicers that use different fields or were copied: give them the same group name. Sync field changes propagates a field swap, Sync filter changes propagates selections. Copying a slicer to another page prompts you to sync; an unsynced copy filters independently.

Two limits to remember. A page or report filter cannot exclude one visual: use a slicer plus Edit interactions → None. And neither filters nor slicers secure data; that is row-level security.

Practical example

Why it works: Sync decides who is filtered, Visible only decides who sees the control. Page 4 has neither, so it keeps its own context. A report-level filter would hit page 4 too; a bookmark per country would be 50 objects to maintain.

Traps

Wrong: A page-level filter carries a selection across pages. Right: it stops at the page. Use a synced slicer or a report-level filter.

Wrong: Make one bookmark per slicer value to persist selections. Right: bookmarks do not persist selections; sync the slicer.

Wrong: A report-level filter can skip one page. Right: it hits every page. Sync only the pages you want.
