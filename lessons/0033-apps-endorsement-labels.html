Lesson 0033

Distribute and govern content: apps, endorsement, sensitivity labels

Manage and secureAbout 20 min

After this lesson you can pick a distribution method, publish or update an app with the right audience, endorse content, and protect it with sensitivity labels.

Why this matters for the exam

Distribution and governance questions are a staple of the Manage and secure domain. The exam rewards one habit: name the exact mechanism (update the app, certify, label) instead of a vague, heavier alternative such as a new app or RLS.

Recall first

A user must publish the app and add colleagues with least privilege. Which role?

Show answer

Member. Contributors cannot publish a new app or add users.

Core idea

Ways to distribute: workspace roles (collaborators), sharing single items, an app (curated, read-only, many consumers), embedding in Teams or SharePoint, subscriptions (email), and Build or Analyze in Excel for self-service.

An app contains only the items you select. Audiences show different content to different groups in one app. After changing workspace content, choose Update app; do not create a second app. App users cannot edit layouts and RLS still applies. Share with users, security groups, mail-enabled security groups, distribution lists or Microsoft 365 groups, or the whole organisation. Terms: app, audience.

Endorsement is a quality signal, not access control. Promoted: any contributor can promote. Certified: only reviewers the admin authorises. Certified items rank first in search and discovery. See endorsement.

Sensitivity labels (Microsoft Purview Information Protection) are applied in Desktop or the service. They flow downstream (model, report, dashboard, and new content built from labelled items) and persist with encryption on export to Excel, PowerPoint and PDF. RLS and OLS do not protect exported files; labels do. See sensitivity label.The lineage view shows source, model, report and dashboard dependencies. Deployment pipelines move content Development, Test, Production.

Practical example

Board members need one dashboard and three reports, read-only, on powerbi.com.

  1. In the workspace, choose Create app and include exactly those four items.
  2. Set the audience to the Board security group.
  3. Label the content "Confidential".
  4. Later, a new page is added: choose Update app.

Why it works: the app limits what consumers see, the label keeps exported PDFs encrypted, and updating avoids a duplicate app and a new URL.

Traps

Wrong: A new report page needs a new app or a changed audience. Right: Just Update app.

Wrong: RLS stops exported PDFs leaking. Right: Use sensitivity labels with encryption.

Wrong: Promoted and Certified are the same. Right: Certified is verified by authorised reviewers and listed first.
