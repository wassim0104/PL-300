Workspace roles, sharing and Build permission

Manage and secureAbout 20 min

After this lesson you can grant the least-privilege role or item permission for a stated need (view, edit, publish an app, add users, build on a model).

Why this matters for the exam

Manage and secure Power BI is a full exam domain, and "which role or permission, with least privilege" is its most common question shape. It appeared repeatedly in practice questions, usually with Member versus Contributor or Build versus a workspace role as the deciding detail.

Core idea

Four workspace roles, from most to least power. Roles in workspaces

RoleCan doCannot
AdminEverything, including delete the workspace and manage Admins-
MemberAdd members or lower roles, publish and update the app, change app permissions, allow resharing, plus all Contributor tasksDelete the workspace, manage Admins
ContributorCreate, edit and delete content, publish from Desktop, schedule refreshAdd users, publish a new app, change app permissions
ViewerView and interactEdit anything

Roles can go to users, security groups, Microsoft 365 groups and distribution lists. A person in several groups gets the highest role. Row-level security applies only to Viewers (and app or shared-item consumers); Admin, Member and Contributor bypass it.RLS in Fabric docs. More in Fabric workspace roles.

Item-level access is separate: when you share a report or dashboard, or manage permissions on a model, you set Read, Reshare and Build. Build lets a user create new reports on the model, even in other workspaces, use Analyze in Excel and query over XMLA. Reshare only passes sharing on. See Build permission in the glossary.

Two rules of thumb. "Build reports on model X in their own workspace" is Build on X, never a role in X's workspace. "Read-only for many people" is an app or a group share, never Member or Contributor.

Practical example

Finance analysts must explore the HR semantic model in Excel without seeing any other HR workspace content.

  1. Open the HR model's Manage permissions.
  2. Grant the finance group Build on that model only.
  3. They use Analyze in Excel. RLS on the model still filters them, because they are not Admin, Member or Contributor.

Why it works: Build is scoped to one item. A workspace role would expose reports, dashboards and other models.

Traps

Wrong: Contributor can publish the app or add users. Right: That needs Member.

Wrong: Admin is the safe choice. Right: Admin over-privileges when Member is enough.

Wrong: Viewer can be limited to selected items. Right: Viewer sees all workspace content. Use item sharing or an app for selected items.
